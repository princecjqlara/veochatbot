# Compact form-first conversation

This release replaces the introductory offer and four-bubble summary layout in the earlier October 18 release.

The introduction has three bubbles: a brief answer to the current inquiry, the complete four-field form, then one missing-field question immediately after the form. After partial answers, the bot sends at most a short answer and a separate question for one missing field. It saves answers given naturally in chat and does not resend the form. Optional discovery and unsolicited free offers wait until the required details are complete.

Conversational answers are limited to two brief sentences and 180 characters. Combined paid-price and free-concept/script inquiries preserve both answers within that budget; shortening a verbose pricing sentence cannot silently remove the free-option answer. The complete summary preserves every verified value and splits into two readable groups: business name/location, then goal/contact number. The next bubble offers a free concept or free script, or confirms the already chosen request and team handoff. A direct customer question on the completing turn still receives a brief answer. Unchanged summaries are not repeated.

The generation validator rejects multiple package prices and retries instead of sending a price catalog. The renderer also replaces a detected catalog with a scope-based explanation. A verified selected-option rate and its deposit/balance remain allowed. Final paid-video quotes depend on confirmed requirements and scope. Free concept and free script require no payment, quote, or purchase.

The reply and reminder sanitizer removes AI prefaces, standalone provider signatures, common AI watermark text, code fences, Markdown decoration, and invisible formatting characters. It applies to model messages containing the form as well as ordinary copy while retaining the form's numbered fields and colons. This change concerns generated chat text; it does not alter portfolio media files.

Runtime prompts retain custom Page guidance while sending the authoritative intake policy once, rather than appending several saved copies of it. All five saved prompt fields adopt `Compact form-first conversation policy (2026-10-18):`. The rollout backs up settings and preserves bot switches, schedules, contact facts, holds, and delivery guards. Existing duplicate-reminder suppression and sent-prefix checkpoints remain active. Previews may correctly suppress a reminder when both model drafts repeat prior copy.
