# Follow-up repetition and split messaging

Ordinary answers, informational offers, follow-up questions, and completed summaries split into natural Messenger bubbles. Only the blank four-field fill-up form stays together. The introductory free offer and the missing-field question are separate bubbles. Completed summaries split by field while retaining all values, and later formatting preserves those boundaries. Automatic formatting no longer collapses ordinary replies into two bubbles. The form-first, four-required-field, free concept/script, and scope-based paid pricing rules remain in place.

Duplicate detection compares normalized copy, including politeness changes, reordered words, an old sentence embedded in a paragraph, and moved bubble boundaries. It preserves different numbers, negation, and distinct summary fields. Replies remove repeated copy within their draft. Scheduled reminders additionally compare against the conversation and the most recent delivered follow-up records for that same Page and contact, covering delayed Messenger history. A second comparison before delivery catches a reminder that was delivered during generation. After two rejected duplicate drafts, that reminder job is cancelled rather than starting another five-minute retry loop; other configured reminders remain eligible.

Each delivered follow-up bubble checkpoints its Messenger message ID and the delivered text before the next send. Stale jobs with a recorded delivery are not replayed. Partial failures retain the sent prefix and remain terminal. This reduces replay risk; an external send and its database write cannot provide an absolute exactly-once guarantee if the process fails between them.

Summary detection recognizes a bounded block of field bubbles in either order because adjacent Messenger bubbles can share timestamps. Actual corrections or an explicit request can produce another summary. Model-written summaries are removed before the application's verified summary, preserving a separate useful next-step answer.
