# Form incentive and sentence splitting

This release supersedes the merged two-sentence answer layout in the earlier compact-intake release. Ordinary sentences now arrive in separate Messenger bubbles, including lowercase sentences and single line breaks. Decimal prices and common abbreviations are preserved. The full blank form alone stays intact; completed summary values retain their two readable groups.

The introduction answers the current FAQ briefly, explains once that the required details let the team prepare a free concept or free script with no fee, sends the full four-field form in one message, and asks one missing-field question immediately in the next message. The incentive precedes the form. It does not ask the customer to choose a free option before collecting details. Partial answers advance only missing fields. Completed details produce the summary, then the selected-request handoff or free next-step choice. Price inquiries receive an applicable verified rate or scope explanation, never a full price catalog.

Duplicate checks now normalize common request synonyms and paki wording. Reply rendering checks the supplied Page history rather than just its last eight bubbles. It removes repeated pitches and acknowledgements after a customer response, while allowing a factual FAQ answer when explicitly requested again. After a completed form, filtering a repeated free-choice question does not fall back to that same question; the bot uses a brief acknowledgement. Existing reminder checks against conversation and delivered follow-up records remain active, with repeated drafts retried or suppressed. These copy checks cannot provide an absolute guarantee against every possible semantic paraphrase or external delivery race.

Text cleanup removes standalone AI labels, Markdown/backticks, dash-style bullets, ASCII dash separators, and em/en dashes from generated prose. Literal hyphens in URLs, phone numbers, and customer-provided summary values retain their meaning.
