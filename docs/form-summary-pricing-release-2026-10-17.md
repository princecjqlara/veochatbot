# Form-first intake, completed summary, and scope-based pricing

The conversation prioritizes the four required details: Business Name, Business Location, Goal ng video, and Contact Number. A brief factual answer may precede the form. The full blank form stays alone in one message. The following message introduces the existing free concept/free script options as information and ends with one specific missing-field question. Customers are not asked to choose an offer before completing their details.

Partial answers and corrections are retained. Replies and scheduled reminders ask one still-missing field, rotate an ignored question, and defer optional duration, budget, style, and package selection. An early pricing question receives an applicable verified reference rate or an explanation that pricing depends on scope, followed by a missing-field question.

When all four fields are complete, the application constructs a summary with their actual answers before the next-step message, optional scope questions, pricing discussion, or handoff. The summary remains intact through downstream formatting, including long values and legacy numeric message limits. Supplied Page history prevents repeating an unchanged summary. Corrections produce an updated summary; a customer can explicitly request it again. The form and summary have explicit exceptions to the ordinary short-reply formatting rules.

Final paid-video pricing depends on requirements and confirmed scope. Rates and package facts must come from verified knowledge or a relevant confirmed quote. Free concept and free script have no fee and require no purchase. Replies and reminders reject and regenerate wording that seeks a quote or payment for these free outputs. Free personalized videos and unapproved offers remain prohibited. A completed form alone does not establish intent; a completed form plus an explicit request hands intake to the team. Staff ownership and stop rules retain precedence. The introduction extracts the full question before bounding ordinary copy, avoiding a cut-off phrase being attached to the question.
