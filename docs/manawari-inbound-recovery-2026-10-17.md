# Manawari Messenger inbound recovery and conversational replies

Restarting a stale contact stop restored a manually replayed reply but did not prove organic webhook delivery. The app-level Page callback subscription was refreshed with its existing callback, version, and field set; unrelated subscriptions were preserved.

The minute follow-up worker now checks explicitly opted-in Pages for recent missing inbound messages before reminders. Manawari is the only Page being opted in. Activation time and a 15-minute lookback exclude historical backlog. Each tick inspects the newest conversation batch, a bounded older cursor batch, and up to three active contacts independently of campaign scanning. Only the newest unanswered customer text/image event is eligible, with its actual message ID and original timestamp. A newer Page response excludes that conversation. Recovery dispatches one event per tick through the ordinary signed webhook, preserving opt-outs, staff ownership, saved detail extraction, duplicate reply claims, generation, and per-bubble delivery checks. Exclusive expiring Page leases and durable event attempt records prevent concurrent recovery and bound retries to three. Read and dispatch failures remain visible in recovery state.

Recovery reduces the impact of missing callbacks; it is a bounded fallback dependent on the minute scheduler and Meta reads, not a guarantee of instant delivery. Existing campaigns and other Page chatbot switches remain as configured.

Commercial intake copy now explicitly uses natural Taglish and prioritizes the four required fields. Its form is exactly four numbered labels in a standalone message. Answer/question copy and the introductory free concept/script offer stay separate from the form even with old numeric message caps. After partial answers the form is not repeated. Recent Page questions inform a deterministic guard: repeated or already-answered field questions move to another missing field, or vary wording when only one field remains. Verified factual answers can still be repeated when requested. Automatic downstream formatting now preserves up to two ordinary messages instead of silently recombining them into one.

Validation: full Vitest suite, TypeScript, scoped ESLint, and scratch-schema SQL verification of explicit opt-in, disabled-Page exclusion, exclusive leases, expiry, and service-only access. Production preview scenarios cover an English inquiry answered in Taglish, standalone form, partial details, repeated inquiry, and follow-up. Production callback health probes contain no customer events and are not counted as evidence of organic inbound delivery.
