# Inquiry, sales and cohort calendar

The calendar keeps all permitted VEO Pages and retained historical inquiry dates visible. Selecting a date shows two sets of results:

* **Activity on the date:** new inquiries; first verified order payments (Closed Deals); verified full payments (Done Deals); completed-order value after refunds; actual commission; cash receipts; recorded ad spend; declared availability; team quota progress, remaining work and surplus; daily message response rate; attributed employee results.
* **The inquiry cohort through the report time:** clients who first inquired on that date, later recorded conversions, verified paying clients, later receipts and completions, actual recognized commission, original acquisition-date spend, return on ad spend, acquisition cost and recorded net after salary and ads. Deposits and balances do not create separate acquired clients.

The same order can have its inquiry, Closed and Done milestones on different Philippine dates. Its later financial outcomes continue updating the original inquiry cohort. Daily completion value and cash receipts remain distinct. Conversion-stage counts and verified financial deals are separate; a stage or image alone cannot verify money. New-period cohort revenue requires the Converted contact and its verified order records. Currency totals are separate and incompatible currencies do not produce a combined return ratio.

Mean and average completed-order value are the same statistic. Median is calculated over individual completed-order values, using verified net values after refunds. Whole-day commission follows the original frozen targets and 35%/40% rules. Exact commission is allocated first to Pages and then to orders with largest remainders, so cohort and Page shares reconcile to the worker's daily commission. Archived financial records remain read-only; historical analytics do not create historical salary, quota or capacity entitlements. Missing retained salary records produce an unavailable net figure rather than a modeled 40% allowance.

Conversion checkpoints show cumulative recorded conversion and verified payer rates within 1, 7, 14 and 30 days of each first inquiry. Windows still in progress are labelled. The report also shows mean/median time from inquiry to qualification, first verified payment and verified full payment. Unknown conversion timestamps remain explicit.

Demographic tables show recorded age bands, gender, location and industry, including unknown values in the percentage denominator. Manual corrections take priority. Supported extracted answers may fill missing fields; names and photos never supply inferred age or gender. Top objections count distinct clients with evidence-backed saved profile facts, with profile coverage shown. Current stage counts distinguish Not Qualified, Qualified, Converted, Order Created and other pending states.

The optional 30-day outlook uses older same-Page inquiry cohorts with at least 30 days of complete recorded conversation history. It conditions on remaining unconverted peers at the observed cohort age, requires at least 30 comparable peers and reports a Wilson sampling range. Potential order value holds the observed completed-order mean constant as a separate assumption. An ended 30-day window is not a lifetime limit on later conversions. Insufficient source history produces no invented estimate.

Daily response rate uses incoming messages on that Philippine date that received a later Page message before that day's midnight; both human and bot Page replies qualify. Migration 026 retains only message identity, direction and timestamps from the existing event journal and outbound records. It stores no message text or image bytes. New events continue updating this metadata even after processed raw webhook payloads expire. Dates before retained metadata coverage show partial/unavailable daily rates. Saved whole-conversation cohort reply rates are labelled separately and do not stand in for historical daily response rates.

Members and managers remain blocked from the calendar. Owner and Team Leaders retain current Page and branch restrictions. All-Pages reporting reads bounded Page slices and bisects date ranges on database API timeouts. Merging deduplicates orders, payment-day totals, employee snapshots and forecast references; response counts sum across distinct Pages. A failed slice fails the report instead of producing partial totals presented as complete.

Apply migrations 026–027 with the workspace release script, run the calendar SQL/calculation/loader/route/rendering tests and production build, then verify the production calendar with `scripts/verify-veo-calendar.mjs`. The probe checks historical all-Page inquiry counts, later receipts, monetary availability, roles, Page filters and current-day reporting without creating sales or dispatching messages.

Migration 028 also corrects a release deduplication issue found by the complete regression suite: multiple unfinished orders for one contact must release that contact exactly once during staff removal. The release selection uses a statement-stable timestamp, preserving financial history and avoiding duplicate claim inserts.
