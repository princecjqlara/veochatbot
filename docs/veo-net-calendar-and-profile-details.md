# Net calendar and contact auto-fill

The VEO calendar defaults to **daily net profit/loss** shading. Green is positive, red negative, white break-even and gray unavailable costs. The scale uses absolute magnitudes so all-loss months shade correctly. A financial shading currency is selected explicitly; currencies are never added together. Activity and eventual inquiry-cohort net shading remain available.

Daily net = verified completion-day order revenue after refunds − actual daily commission − that date's ads. Cohort net uses the cohort's verified receipts through report time, actual commission on completed orders and its acquisition-date ads. Future commission can reduce an unfinished cohort's return. Net ROAS = (revenue − actual commission) / ad spend, with 1× break-even. Zero ad spend, missing/partial ad coverage, unknown historical salary or incompatible revenue currencies prevent a ROAS. Other costs and taxes are excluded from the displayed net figure. Retained historical salary is never invented.

Calendar cells display net profit/loss and the 35%/40% rates actually applied to workers with completions. Mixed rates stay distinct, including their currency. The selected-date salary card and existing employee table show the rate breakdown. Zero-sale dates do not present a hypothetical default rate as paid salary.

Contact profiles reuse their existing lead-stage badge, lifetime-value panel, purchase/payment history, and purpose/problem sections. Purpose and problems are expanded for visibility. The existing verified-history panel now summarizes recorded paid offers and average recorded prices for distinct orders with verified payment; it also shows net paid per order after refunds. Deposits and balances do not become separate purchases, unallocated payments affect lifetime value without creating an order average, and currencies stay separate. Complete payment details remain in the existing Payments section. Former sellers in the permitted branch retain their financial history.

Profile auto-fill extends the existing private demographic queue. It extracts buying purpose, problems, explicit preferred contact time, Page-configured customer details and up to 20 additional per-contact targets. Customer message IDs and exact quotations support each AI value. Existing manual corrections (including intentional Unknown), saved form answers and supported historical facts take precedence. Aliases prevent duplicate targets such as Phone / Customer phone or Business name / Company name. Financial/control fields are not AI targets; paid offers and stages use their authoritative records.

Adding a target requests extraction from saved customer messages; it does not alter the Page-wide questions or completion rules of the live chatbot. Those remain configurable in AI Chatbot settings. New inbound events enqueue updates automatically. The worker uses at most 24 saved customer excerpts/new-period messages; no full-history Meta imports are enabled. Missing information stays Unknown. An explicit contact-time preference takes priority over the existing activity estimate; estimates remain labeled with saved confidence, and no default noon time is presented as a known preference.

Migration `202611070008_veo_contact_profile_fields.sql` adds target metadata and tracks pending manual extraction versions. New messages cannot postpone a pending manual request, and a new manual request during a lease is queued promptly after that lease. Automatic updates retain their debounce/cooldown. Previously enrolled profiles are requeued for the upgraded extractor; contacts without a profile job remain opt-in. Existing private functions, leases, deduplication, Page revocation checks, archive data, payment records and Telegram delivery are preserved. Polling uses lightweight job metadata and reloads the complete profile only after new analysis completes.
