# Receipts, staff obligations, funded quotas and creative reporting

The **Receipts** workspace tab accepts Closed Deal and Done Deal evidence. A **Closed Deal** requires the current lead stage to be **Qualified or Converted**, with no order creation required. Its receipt amount and payment time are retained as a separate Closed Deal record; that declared amount is not verified revenue or lifetime value. A **Done Deal** requires a Qualified or Converted lead, an attributed created order, and verified settlement. The Closed Deal payment plus additional payments must match the total deal price within **PHP 2 in either direction**. Other currencies must match exactly. Each receipt displays missing steps and a **Recheck** action. Staff see their own records and supervisors see their permitted branch and Pages. Photos stay private, expire after seven days and are never stored as database bytes.

Before uploading, staff select a client and see the current lead stage. Closed Deal submission asks for its reported payment amount, currency, Philippine payment time and photo. In Done Deal mode, staff select the earlier Closed Deal, create/select the matching order and its **deal price**, and enter the **additional payment amount** and reference. Explicit financial verification imports the earlier payment once and reconciles the total atomically. Already recorded payments must be matched instead of added again. A difference above the tolerance rolls back the settlement; unchanged retries keep a stable request ID. Zero additional payment is allowed when earlier verified receipts already cover the price. Notes can be added and edited with an audit record, without changing financial records.

The receipt page also shows the selected seller's current Philippine-day Closed and Done quota counts, remaining work, current commission rate, and both 35% and 40% earnings scenarios. Both personal quotas are required for 40%; otherwise the rate is 35%. Commission uses verified net receipts from completed orders, with no salary from an order-free Closed Deal alone. Today's rate remains provisional until Philippine midnight. Normalized sales facts preserve one Closed event when an order is later linked; capacity, calendar, rankings, salary and Jaucian share those facts. Money follows current Page access; quota qualification uses the worker's complete day. Contact payment history uses the same approved settlement tolerance.

Closed Deal forwarding uses a stable Closed Deal reference and rechecks the Qualified/Converted stage and Page/seller access. It does not require an order or pretend that the reported amount is verified money. Done Deal forwarding requires the created order and verified settlement within the allowed tolerance. Jaucian distinguishes reported Closed amounts from verified payments, and uses a separate idempotent milestone key. Linking an order does not change the Closed delivery identity or count the sale again. Telegram posts never create ledger sales. Jaucian's existing scheduled snapshots remind mapped workers about missing Done Deal steps; there is no manual dispatcher call or separate reminder bot.

**Warnings** shows current receipt salary holds, overdue projects without an explanation, missing Telegram mappings and unanswered posted active-for-tomorrow polls. A warning does not automatically deduct salary or fire staff. A project marked finished while waiting only for the client's balance is not staff overdue. A poll reminder needs a real posted poll, a currently verified mapped human, fresh sync data and an actual missing vote; missing polls or stale data stay unknown. Formal missed-vote warnings wait until 22:00 PHT and at least two hours after posting. Current warnings clear when the obligation is resolved; the underlying receipts, audited actions and Telegram observations remain retained.

Tomorrow attendance is read from Jaucian's existing non-anonymous Telegram poll. Workspace tomorrow controls are removed. A vote supersedes former parallel workspace declarations, and withdrawn votes return to unknown. Current-day supervisor corrections remain audited. Contacts show both per-order and mean **CD to DD time**, using the first verified payment through full settlement; immediate full payment is zero hours.

**Capacity** remains the established best-three eligible Done Deal days over the previous fourteen completed Philippine dates, at 80% rounded down with minimum/default one. **Operating quota** is separately limited by existing paid unfinished clients and funded new-client capacity. Meta daily campaign/ad-set budgets are allocated once across active ads, including placements outside VEO; lifetime budgets never become invented daily budgets. Observed CPA uses prior complete Philippine ad-spend days and actual paying clients. Missing, stale or unavailable budgets/CPA retain the proven-capacity target. Confirmed no-ad days with no existing work can have operating quota zero; this creates no salary and still requires at least one real Closed and Done Deal to qualify for 40%. Daily snapshots are immutable after creation, completed dates are unchanged, and shared Page/team plans use the same allocation. These planning reads never edit Meta ad budgets.

**Ads Performance** adds a Page-scoped ad/creative report: attributed inquiries, qualified/not-qualified/current converted stages, conversion rate, distinct paying clients, orders, verified gross receipts, paid-order average, actual commission, net profit/loss, acquisition and purchase costs, average daily spend, inquiry-to-conversion delay, delivery history, demographics and industry/sub-industry. Later outcomes stay attached to the original inquiry cohort. Unknown demographics remain in the denominator. Retained salary, missing spend, incompatible currencies and zero denominators remain unavailable rather than guessed. Daily graphs have a matching numeric table. Daily frequency comes from Meta; exact selected-ad range frequency is requested separately because daily unique reach cannot be summed into an unduplicated period audience. Running age is explicitly based on first recorded delivery, not proof of uninterrupted history.

New read paths and existing operations use bounded transient-read retry with current server authorization on each attempt. Parameter-specific database plans and indexes bound retained-record reads. Writes are not blindly retried after a timeout. Migrations prepare the compatible functions and private tables before the application workflow is enabled; Jaucian's compatible receiver is deployed first. Archives and retained financial records are preserved.

Meta fields were checked against the official [Ads Insights SDK definitions](https://github.com/meta/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/main/facebook_business/adobjects/adsinsights.py) and [ad-set SDK definitions](https://github.com/meta/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/main/facebook_business/adobjects/adset.py).

Operating Page allocation prioritizes supplied Pages: a fresh, confirmed no-ad Page with no paid unfinished client work receives zero supply weight while another assigned Page has work. Unknown supply keeps the saved weight. Production capacity remains separately allocated, and largest-remainder rounding still conserves the worker's operating target. A completely unsupplied day uses its frozen target; the next daily snapshot can reduce that target to zero.
