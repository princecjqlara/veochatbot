# Receipts, staff obligations, funded quotas and creative reporting

The **Receipts** workspace tab accepts Closed Deal and Done Deal evidence only after **Check Facebook requirements** passes. Submit stays disabled until then. Submission performs a new bounded Facebook read and rejects incomplete evidence **before photo storage**. A **Closed Deal** requires an accessible Page-authored **Qualified or Converted** stage notice and an eligible current VEO stage, with no order creation required. Its receipt amount and payment time are retained as a separate Closed Deal record; that declared amount is not verified revenue or lifetime value. A **Done Deal** additionally requires the matching Facebook invoice in this check, its linked attributed order, and verified settlement in the VEO payment ledger. The Closed Deal payment plus additional payments must match the total deal price within **PHP 2 in either direction**. Other currencies must match exactly. Team and seller Telegram mappings must be complete. Staff see their own records and supervisors see their permitted branch and Pages. Photos stay private, expire after seven days and are never stored as database bytes.

**Missing, partial, timed-out or unavailable Facebook evidence blocks submission.** The Conversations API exposes administrative notices, not guaranteed access to every Business Suite UI change or bank/payment status. No stage notice means unknown, even if VEO was manually marked Qualified. The screen shows the latest accessible stage notice, its event date, check time, coverage and exact blockers. It never treats an order notice, customer message or uploaded receipt as payment verification. The existing chronological stage sync preserves newer manual corrections. A private per-contact snapshot contains parsed metadata only; failed checks invalidate earlier success and older overlapping requests cannot replace newer results. Database save/recheck functions enforce the same requirements with a two-minute freshness limit, so forged browser evidence and the older photo upload path cannot bypass them. Incomplete new receipts are rejected, rather than added to a review queue. Historical held receipts remain retained and can be rechecked after completing the requirements.

The workspace delivery worker and Jaucian's authenticated last-minute eligibility read refresh Facebook again before handoff or temporary photo access. Provider outages retry automatically without creating a new sale, duplicate milestone or manual review requirement. Changed stage, invoice, settlement, attribution or Page grants block forwarding. Verified financial history is retained when a provider is temporarily unavailable.

With no team topic override, Jaucian uses that existing group's **Close Deal** and **Done Deal** topics for receipt confirmations and its **announcements** topic for team reports. Reports run at **08:00**, **16:00**, and **00:10 Philippine time**; the midnight report closes the previous financial date, with current client status and warnings. Reports include canonical VEO staff warnings, unpaid clients, overdue work, and recorded delay explanations. A finished project waiting only for the client balance is not staff overdue. Missing or stale poll data never establishes a missed vote. Financial amounts remain in the role-protected app. Successful service authentication alone does not mean deliveries are enabled: approved team destinations, verified numeric staff identities and eligible source records are still required. The owner connection screen distinguishes Closed/Done confirmations, reports and polls by their actual outbox metadata.

The same snapshots show the remaining team and personal Closed/Done quotas, acknowledge both personal targets when reached, and display the authoritative daily 35% or 40% rate with provisional/final status. No-ad planning quota zero never grants a commission without real qualifying sales. VEO's scheduled workforce pull imports tomorrow availability from Jaucian's recorded non-anonymous Telegram poll votes and verified numeric identities; it does not collect a separate in-app tomorrow declaration. Unmapped identities, absent polls, withdrawn votes and stale observations remain unknown.

VEO stage sync does not infer a lead-stage change from an invoice notice. A selected client's earlier inferred Order Created stage is restored to the freshly observed explicit stage only when the exact inferred invoice message is present in that same Facebook read. This correction is audited; manual changes and explicit Order Created lead-stage notices are preserved. No bulk historical repair or conversation import runs.

A fresh Graph scan cannot override a newer conflicting Facebook stage already retained from a signed event. Conflicts, including ambiguous stages at the same timestamp, block submission and forwarding until consistent evidence is available. Save recovery matches the original client, seller and receipt type. It never returns a different client's receipt after a request-ID collision. A lost save response retains its temporary photo while the outcome is uncertain; unused retry uploads are removed only after checking that no receipt references them. The normal seven-day cleanup still applies.

Receipt submission starts with **Search client** and **Pick client**. The app checks that client's open Closed Deals for the selected seller and shows their saved down payments. Staff click **Turn this Closed Deal into Done Deal** on the correct deal. They can also start a separate **New Closed Deal / down payment** or an immediate fully paid deal. Changing the client search clears the earlier selection and payment inputs. Multiple deals can coexist without replacing earlier amounts.

Orders are created in **Facebook Business Suite**. In Done Deal mode, staff click **Fetch Facebook orders** and select an accessible Page-authored order notice. The invoice ID and **total price** are parsed from the actual Facebook notice and stored server-side; customer prose and client-supplied totals cannot become an imported order. Staff select the seller and deal to link. Invoice imports are idempotent, current Page/branch access is checked again, and a changed price on a linked invoice blocks further payments for review. Existing local orders remain available. The standalone create-order form is replaced with the Business Suite fetch flow.

The fetch uses Meta's [Conversations API](https://www.postman.com/meta/messenger-platform-api/folder/22794852-255610cd-47f5-4f4d-b3fa-71aec360be9a). It checks at most five currently available message pages for the selected client, stops at the launch cutoff, and stores only parsed order metadata. It does not import conversation history, restart backfills or treat missing Meta details as complete coverage. There is no assumption that every Business Suite order is exposed by Meta. Inaccessible notices and incomplete scans are shown explicitly.

The Closed Deal amount field is **Down payment**. The Done Deal amount field is **How much the client sent** and starts blank, so staff enter the actual payment received rather than an assumed remaining balance. The **Total price** stays visible. The system adds the earlier down payment and verified payments, calculates the remaining balance and checks the price difference. Explicit financial verification imports the earlier payment once and reconciles the total atomically. Already recorded payments must be matched instead of added again. A difference above the tolerance rolls back settlement; unchanged retries keep a stable request ID. Enter zero only when earlier receipts already cover the price. Saved Done receipts show **Client sent** and the total price. Notes remain editable with an audit record.

**Delete receipt** is available on each visible receipt card. Members can delete their own seller receipts; managers can delete their own and their branch members' receipts; team leaders and Owner can delete within their permitted branch and Pages. Server authorization is checked on every deletion, and a reason is required. Deletion hides the receipt, blocks pending forwarding, makes notes/rechecks read-only and schedules its temporary photo for cleanup. The receipt metadata and deletion audit remain retained. Already posted Telegram summaries remain in Telegram. Verified orders, payments and commissions are preserved.

For an unlinked Closed Deal without a verified payment, the delete form additionally offers **Also cancel this mistaken Closed Deal**. This is an explicit choice: it voids that order-free sale, excludes it from quota/report facts, and queues audited salary reconciliation where a daily rate may change. Other active receipt copies must be removed first. A linked or paid deal cannot be cancelled through receipt deletion; financial corrections retain the existing verified-payment/refund workflow. Employee removal remains separately available in Accounts & Teams with its established hierarchy permissions.

The receipt page also shows the selected seller's current Philippine-day Closed and Done quota counts, remaining work, current commission rate, and both 35% and 40% earnings scenarios. Both personal quotas are required for 40%; otherwise the rate is 35%. Commission uses verified net receipts from completed orders, with no salary from an order-free Closed Deal alone. Today's rate remains provisional until Philippine midnight. Normalized sales facts preserve one Closed event when an order is later linked; capacity, calendar, rankings, salary and Jaucian share those facts. Money follows current Page access; quota qualification uses the worker's complete day. Contact payment history uses the same approved settlement tolerance.

Closed Deal forwarding uses a stable Closed Deal reference and rechecks the Qualified/Converted stage and Page/seller access. It does not require an order or pretend that the reported amount is verified money. Done Deal forwarding requires the created order and verified settlement within the allowed tolerance. Jaucian distinguishes reported Closed amounts from verified payments, and uses a separate idempotent milestone key. Linking an order does not change the Closed delivery identity or count the sale again. Telegram posts never create ledger sales. Jaucian's existing scheduled snapshots remind mapped workers about missing Done Deal steps; there is no manual dispatcher call or separate reminder bot.

**Warnings** shows current receipt salary holds, overdue projects without an explanation, missing Telegram mappings and unanswered posted active-for-tomorrow polls. A warning does not automatically deduct salary or fire staff. A project marked finished while waiting only for the client's balance is not staff overdue. A poll reminder needs a real posted poll, a currently verified mapped human, fresh sync data and an actual missing vote; missing polls or stale data stay unknown. Formal missed-vote warnings wait until 22:00 PHT and at least two hours after posting. Current warnings clear when the obligation is resolved; the underlying receipts, audited actions and Telegram observations remain retained.

Tomorrow attendance is read from Jaucian's existing non-anonymous Telegram poll. Workspace tomorrow controls are removed. A vote supersedes former parallel workspace declarations, and withdrawn votes return to unknown. Current-day supervisor corrections remain audited. Contacts show both per-order and mean **CD to DD time**, using the first verified payment through full settlement; immediate full payment is zero hours.

**Capacity** remains the established best-three eligible Done Deal days over the previous fourteen completed Philippine dates, at 80% rounded down with minimum/default one. **Operating quota** is separately limited by existing paid unfinished clients and funded new-client capacity. Meta daily campaign/ad-set budgets are allocated once across active ads, including placements outside VEO; lifetime budgets never become invented daily budgets. Observed CPA uses prior complete Philippine ad-spend days and actual paying clients. Missing, stale or unavailable budgets/CPA retain the proven-capacity target. Confirmed no-ad days with no existing work can have operating quota zero; this creates no salary and still requires at least one real Closed and Done Deal to qualify for 40%. Daily snapshots are immutable after creation, completed dates are unchanged, and shared Page/team plans use the same allocation. These planning reads never edit Meta ad budgets.

**Ads Performance** adds a Page-scoped ad/creative report: attributed inquiries, qualified/not-qualified/current converted stages, conversion rate, distinct paying clients, orders, verified gross receipts, paid-order average, actual commission, net profit/loss, acquisition and purchase costs, average daily spend, inquiry-to-conversion delay, delivery history, demographics and industry/sub-industry. Later outcomes stay attached to the original inquiry cohort. Unknown demographics remain in the denominator. Retained salary, missing spend, incompatible currencies and zero denominators remain unavailable rather than guessed. Daily graphs have a matching numeric table. Daily frequency comes from Meta; exact selected-ad range frequency is requested separately because daily unique reach cannot be summed into an unduplicated period audience. Running age is explicitly based on first recorded delivery, not proof of uninterrupted history.

New read paths and existing operations use bounded transient-read retry with current server authorization on each attempt. Parameter-specific database plans and indexes bound retained-record reads. Writes are not blindly retried after a timeout. Migrations prepare the compatible functions and private tables before the application workflow is enabled; Jaucian's compatible receiver is deployed first. Archives and retained financial records are preserved.

Meta fields were checked against the official [Ads Insights SDK definitions](https://github.com/meta/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/main/facebook_business/adobjects/adsinsights.py) and [ad-set SDK definitions](https://github.com/meta/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/main/facebook_business/adobjects/adset.py).

Operating Page allocation prioritizes supplied Pages: a fresh, confirmed no-ad Page with no paid unfinished client work receives zero supply weight while another assigned Page has work. Unknown supply keeps the saved weight. Production capacity remains separately allocated, and largest-remainder rounding still conserves the worker's operating target. A completely unsupplied day uses its frozen target; the next daily snapshot can reduce that target to zero.
