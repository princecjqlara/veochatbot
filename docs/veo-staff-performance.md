# Staff performance

Open Staff accounts and choose **View staff performance, earnings and graphs**,
or open **Performance** and select an employee. The existing date and Page
filters apply to the profile, graphs, client history and monetary values.
Both endpoints are inclusive Philippine dates, bounded to 93 days since
launch through today. Today is provisional and excluded from daily averages
and best-day comparisons; completed zero-sales employment dates are included.

The profile retains attendance declarations, response-rate coverage, salary,
capacity, quota history and client/order tools. Added insights include:

- Current assigned clients, first-assignment new clients per day and clients
  handled since launch. A returning client is not a new acquisition twice.
- Average CD/DD per day, strongest dates and weekday averages with sample
  counts. No-sales dates and unknown attendance remain distinct.
- Mean, median, fastest and slowest CD-to-DD payment completion, measured on
  orders first Closed in the selected range, including later verified payments.
- Separate video production time from first verified payment to recorded work
  completion, filtered by completion date and attributed editor. Payment
  completion does not imply video delivery.
- Overall and per-Page ranks for the selected dates. Overall rank always uses
  all permitted Pages. Ranks use DD then CD and share ties; zero sales means
  unranked. Existing member self/manager team/leader branch/owner permissions
  are enforced before reporting; member views do not reveal peer rankings.
- Daily clients/CD/DD, weekday, financial and personal-response graphs, plus
  the complete daily data table. A currency selector keeps money separate.

Gross sales are completed order prices before refunds. Staff earnings reuse
the authoritative daily commission calculation. Gross profit here means
verified net completed sales less that staff commission, before ads and
untracked overhead. Existing refunds, salary corrections and Page commission
shares are retained rather than recalculated with a new salary policy.

Estimated net profit subtracts allocated advertising from gross profit. Each
verified Page/day's ad cost is allocated across sellers in proportion to that
day's verified net completed revenue. Largest-remainder apportionment preserves
the original cost down to the cent. Only the selected employee's share is
returned; other sellers' financial rows are not exposed. Page scope uses
current grants and recorded seller history, rather than an invented history
of Page assignments. This is an estimate of personal ad cost, not direct
measurement, and excludes untracked operating expenses.

Ad spend must pass the existing Page/day completeness, PHT alignment,
freshness, duplicate and amount reconciliation checks. Missing costs, mixed
revenue currencies or positive ad spend with no Page revenue make net profit
unavailable. A missing day prevents a misleading partial-period net average.
Verified zero spend is a valid zero. Negative profit remains negative; graph
lines leave gaps where costs are unavailable. Currency exchange is not inferred.

Personal response rate retains the existing definition: assigned-client
inquiry days with an attributed manual reply within 24 hours. Bot replies,
unattributed Business Suite messages and clients assigned to other workers
cannot verify a personal response. Missing message-capture coverage and open
24-hour response windows remain visible in the original profile.

The additive migration changes report functions and adds one assignment-history
index. It does not modify orders, payments, salary, attendance, assignments,
messages or historical receipts. Tests cover financial arithmetic and missing
data, attribution, dates, cost conservation, refund handling, permissions,
turnaround, chart controls, loading and existing profile behavior. Browser
visual review is unavailable in this session; no production financial records
or customer messages are created for release verification.
