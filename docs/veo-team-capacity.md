# Team quotas, combined capacity and Owner marketing reports

Capacity & Quotas now shows the individual worker target and a separate team/branch summary. A manager sees their team; a team leader sees their managers' teams; CJ sees every team. Members see their own team's aggregate results without receiving siblings' salary or payment details. Result counts and historical averages are limited to the viewer's currently assigned Pages.

Full team capacity is the sum of the selling workers' frozen individual targets, including selling managers. Average worker capacity divides that sum by the number of workers. The team quota sums targets for the workers who declared themselves active for that work date. Both Closed Deal and Done Deal team quotas are displayed with results, progress and remaining work. Unknown attendance stays unknown; it does not silently count as active, and a zero quota is never labelled reached.

Daily team roster, capacity and declared participation snapshots are immutable. The regular maintenance job snapshots the current day, while reports can establish missing snapshots on first read. Tomorrow's preview remains live: it sums the current estimates only for confirmed active workers, shows inactive/undeclared worker counts and shows full-team potential separately. Editing tomorrow never rewrites an existing day's snapshot. Team aggregates do not alter the existing individual 35%/40% commission qualification.

The combined team daily average sums each day's verified Done Deals across the visible teams and divides by the number of completed Philippine dates in the previous 14-date window since launch. Zero-output dates are included. The separate best-three team days average uses the top three positive combined dates in that window; fewer than three dates display insufficient history. This descriptive team average does not replace the sum of individual capacity targets. Branch averages are calculated from raw worker/day totals rather than averaging already rounded team averages.

Migration `202611050017_veo_team_capacity.sql` provides the service-only aggregates and immutable daily snapshots. The workspace and Jaucian's authoritative report RPC return the same team figures. The existing Jaucian bot includes team Closed/Done quota progress, full and average capacity, completed-date output averages and tomorrow's participation in its normal scheduled report. Delivery is still scheduled and deduplicated; no test message is dispatched manually.

CJ's navigation restores **Calendar & Ad Spend** and **Ads Performance**, with direct links on the teams dashboard. These use saved, authorized marketing data in the email-only session. Missing/stale data stays labelled; historical pipeline/chat outcomes and the calendar's historical 40% marketing allowance are explicitly separated from verified sales and actual workspace commission. Historical conversation verification is suppressed on the saved performance screen. Existing Page collection remains active and Facebook sign-in remains removed.
