# Telegram groups, Pages and Tokko assignments

Open **VEO workspace > Telegram Groups & Pages**. The Head can fetch the approved VEO groups registered with the existing Jaucian bot, their configured Pages and observed human identities. This is metadata discovery, not permission to join an unapproved group. Observed identities are an incomplete list; saving a person verifies current human membership with Telegram.

Under **Assign group to**, choose a person from **Team Leaders - no manager required**, or choose an optional manager. Choose the fetched group and one or more permitted Pages. Selecting a team leader assigns the group directly to the leader and their direct members. A team may use several groups, and a group may handle several Pages. Each team/Page has one delivery destination. Moving an already routed Page requires the explicit Move Pages option. The Head can configure every branch; team leaders and managers can configure only their own branches and permitted Pages. Group routing never grants access to another Page.

Add each person, including selling managers, to each applicable linked group. Use the same numeric Telegram identity across memberships. Unlinking a group does not fire an account. Leaving one group does not remove a worker who remains in another applicable group; offboarding requires fresh confirmed departure observations for every current applicable membership. Unknown or unavailable membership checks cannot fire anyone.

The existing Jaucian bot supports `/ready`, `/busy`, `/clients`, `/offers`, `/myclients` and `/reassign`. These commands and bound buttons use the same assignments as Tokko. Offers retain the existing client owner until accepted; acceptance makes the recipient Busy and pauses the chatbot. Readiness expires after 30 minutes or Philippine midnight, and offers expire after 15 minutes or the recipient's readiness expiry. There is no second Telegram client owner and no automatic assignment just from becoming Ready. Supervisors can offer clients, or eligible workers can choose available clients.

Client menus and offers are scoped to the group's routed Pages and the account's current branch and Page permissions. The normal scheduled outbox sends replies and offer notifications; access and membership are checked again at delivery. Expired menus require a fresh command. Existing legacy assignment buttons cannot modify a parallel assignment system.

Receipts use the seller's verified membership and the team's destination for the receipt's Page. The strict Facebook lead-stage, order and payment checks still apply. For configured teams, missing Page routing or seller membership blocks submission. A routing or permission change also blocks an already queued receipt from reaching its former destination.

Scheduled performance reports show the group's Page results and client lists. Personal commission qualification and attendance remain one set of records per person across all Pages. Tomorrow's polls use Jaucian's existing active-for-tomorrow topic, one poll per group. The latest valid declaration across applicable groups determines a worker's tomorrow state; duplicate group observations do not duplicate workers or salary.

## Rollout

Migration `202611080007` allows a member to report directly to a team leader, with no manager or initial Page selection required. Each leader has a direct-member team; its totals exclude child managers and their teams.

In **Your Telegram profile**, enter your username and choose **Create Telegram link command**. Send the one-time `/veolink` command yourself to an approved VEO group. Codes expire after 15 minutes and only their hash is stored. Forwarded commands, bots, unknown members and identity changes cannot claim another account. Existing supervisor-verified numeric mappings also work. A typed username is not proof of identity.

The scheduled sync checks verified identities in approved groups. An existing app team leader or manager who is a current Telegram administrator or creator gets their groups and registered, permitted Pages linked automatically. It does not change app roles or move existing Page routes. A supervisor can still configure a group when the leader is not its Telegram administrator. Members receive the routed Pages of groups in their own team, bounded by supervisor permissions. New automatic grants are tracked; confirmed departures or removed routing revoke these grants, while unknown lookups retain access and pre-existing manual grants are preserved. Multiple groups use the same identity and keep personal salary and targets unique.

The app prompts users to confirm their Telegram username at sign-in and keeps a profile reminder visible when a username or verified link is missing. Telegram setup shows automatic sync time and errors. No additional bot or manual message dispatcher is required.

Apply migrations `202611080003` through `202611080007` before deploying the app and bot. Existing single-group teams remain compatible until explicitly configured. Preserve the launch cutoff, archived records, account identities and Page grants. Enable `VEO_TELEGRAM_INTAKE_ENABLED=true` on Jaucian only with the authenticated app bridge deployed; both services continue using the existing tenant and shared integration secret. Keep the current approved chat allowlist.

Run the app workflow/API/UI tests and production build, and Jaucian's `py validate_project.py`. After deployment run `remote_windows.ps1 configure-scheduler --verify-recovery`, `scheduler-status`, and read-only health checks. Do not manually dispatch Telegram messages. Fetching the approved directory is safe after rollout; automatic bindings use existing app roles, current Telegram administrator status and verified identities; supervisors can review or configure remaining mappings.
