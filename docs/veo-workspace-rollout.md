# VEO workspace

The maintained Next.js application and Jaucian use the CRM's verified sales ledger as the sole source of new-period sales. Telegram posts and AI extractions do not create payments. Commission is calculated, never transferred.

## Preparation and launch

1. Run the workspace SQL, authentication, sender, webhook, export, and migration regression suites, `npm run build`, and Jaucian's `py validate_project.py`. Keep its Vercel validation gate and tests in the deployment.
2. Load the existing maintained app environment, then run `node scripts/release-veo-workspace.mjs --inspect` and `--preflight`. The latter rolls back all preparation and checks retained account, page-membership, and financial counts.
3. Apply migrations `202611050010` through `202611050018` with `--apply`. They prepare the workspace, worker authorization, private storage, refund/submission recovery, bounded salary reconciliation, audit corrections, scoped client search, Telegram profile confirmation and team capacity snapshots with live current-day planning. They do not change the launch cutoff. The private photo bucket has a 10 MB storage limit and JPG/PNG/WebP allowlist; the application accepts conversation photos up to 3 MB.
4. Deploy both services with workspace mode disabled. Configure the same random `VEO_JAUCIAN_SECRET` in both services; configure the application's `VEO_JAUCIAN_URL` and Jaucian's `VEO_WORKSPACE_URL`. `scripts/configure-veo-workspace.mjs --prepare` configures these through stdin without logging secret values. Never put the handoff secret in public variables or browser code. The compatible webhook journals post-cutoff events before workspace mode is enabled.
5. Set `VEO_OWNER_USER_ID` to CJ Lara's retained database UUID and run `--launch`. This archives operational records read-only, binds the existing owner identity, retains only their approved VEO page connections, revokes old operational memberships, and stops legacy campaigns, exports and follow-ups. No records or page tokens are deleted. Archived users can be re-enrolled by a supervisor using their existing email; their identity and Facebook binding are retained.
6. Configure `VEO_WORKSPACE_TENANT_ID` and `VEO_WORKSPACE_LAUNCH_AT` in Jaucian from the launch receipt. Set `VEO_APPROVED_TELEGRAM_CHAT_IDS` on the application to the existing approved VEO groups. `scripts/configure-veo-workspace.mjs --enable` reads the saved launch receipt and configures the tenant/cutoff and feature flags on both services. Redeploy both. Keep `VEO_PAGE_SCOPE_ENFORCED=true` and the existing page-runtime ownership enforcement enabled.
7. Run `remote_windows.ps1 configure-scheduler --verify-recovery`, `scheduler-status`, and the public `/api/health`. Verification must not dispatch Telegram messages. Wait for normal scheduled delivery to verify publication.

The application cron `/api/cron/workspace` handles new webhook events, daily commission finalization, handoff retries, and private-photo cleanup every minute. It and other scheduled application routes require the existing `CRON_SECRET`. Historical conversation import, stage-history scans, inbound-history recovery and profile backfills stop in workspace mode. Profiles can still use retained evidence and current Facebook profile data. Sync health distinguishes no recorded events from complete history.

## Access and accounting

Only stored account roles grant access. Owner → team leader → manager → member relationships may skip the team-leader level for a manager directly under the owner. Members always belong to a selling manager. Supervisors can create lower accounts by choosing an eligible parent in their branch. Every account needs at least one currently permitted page when created. Removing a page cascades down that supervisor's branch; every campaign send and export batch checks current authorization.

Workers are members and selling managers. Each order has one seller and one currency. Closed Deal is its first verified receipt; Done Deal is its first full verified settlement. Both require Converted status. Immediate full settlement earns both milestones once. Amounts use integer minor units. Verified payments are immutable; corrections use supervisor-authorized refunds and audited seller changes. Payment references are unique within a page, and running balances reject overpayments and backdated refunds preceding their receipts.

Targets default to one. With three eligible Done Deal dates in the preceding 14 completed Philippine dates, the target is floor(80% × the average of the best three dates), with minimum one. Targets are frozen per worker/date. Tomorrow's declarations change planning only. A worker earns 40% only after meeting both daily targets, otherwise 35%, applied to their completed order totals for that Philippine date. Today is provisional. Finalized ledgers are immutable; later financial, eligibility and attribution corrections produce adjustment rows. Refunds and subsequent repayments adjust the original completion date using verified net funds. A durable queue reconciles changed dates and yesterday, with a single maintenance writer and at most 100 dates per run, rather than rescanning every historical date each minute.

Contact profiles combine retained verified payments and new verified payments by currency, subtract refunds, and label coverage. Manual contact corrections take priority over extracted details; neither is financial verification. Historical orders/payments remain read-only and do not enter new-period targets or commissions.

## Telegram delivery and recovery

CJ explicitly maps each selling team to an existing approved VEO group/topic and each worker to their verified Telegram user ID. No destination is inferred from a name. Jaucian accepts authenticated metadata and persists one outbox action per tenant/order. The normal scheduler re-reads eligibility, access, attribution and destination immediately before fetching a 60-second private photo link and sending the summary. No image bytes or signed photo URLs are stored in either database.

The app deletes photos after confirmed delivery or after seven days. Upload reservations let it clean abandoned photos too. Expired submissions require a fresh upload. Known failures retry; acknowledged Telegram message IDs are checkpointed and reused on completion retries. Telegram supplies no send idempotency key: a timeout after accepting a message can be ambiguous. Such deliveries are held for reconciliation instead of blindly resent, and shown as blocked in the application. Do not clear the hold or resubmit without checking the destination. This preserves duplicate prevention without claiming an impossible exactly-once network guarantee.

Jaucian workspace mode suppresses legacy scheduled actions and archive-derived sales/salary reports, excludes Suno from collection and delivery, and generates mapped daily reports from the authenticated application ledger. Scheduler guard failures still allow otherwise due actions. The public health endpoint remains read-only and fails while unacknowledged delivery holds require reconciliation. Replacement uploads and corrected mappings reset known failed attempts; an existing Telegram acknowledgment is recovered without another send. Blocked or expired submissions cannot regain temporary photo access without a fresh submission.
